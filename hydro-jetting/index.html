Sewer backup: what to do first

A backup shows up at the lowest drain, often a basement floor drain or the bathroom nearest the street. Do not run the washer, dishwasher or showers. Every flush adds to the problem.

Keep people and pets away from standing wastewater, and photograph it for your insurer before cleanup. Then call the 24/7 line so we can plan the right jetter for the pipe size.

When jetting beats cabling

  • A line that clogs again within weeks of snaking.
  • Grease coating a long kitchen or restaurant drain.
  • Roots threaded through a clay or concrete line.
  • Heavy mineral scale narrowing older metal pipes.
  • A main line that needs cleaning before a lining or repair.

How a jetting visit works

Camera first

We run a camera to see the blockage and the pipe condition, since jetting a cracked line can make it worse.

Choose the nozzle

A rooting nozzle cuts roots. A scouring nozzle clears grease and scale.

Jet and clean

We work from the cleanout toward the main while flushing debris downstream.

Verify

A second camera pass confirms the pipe wall is clear and shows any damage.

A camera pass after cleaning matters because a freshly jetted line often reveals what caused the blockage, such as a cracked joint or a sag. If the camera finds damage, the next step is a repair discussion under sewer line repair.

Commercial strips such as the Black Horse Pike corridor include food-service kitchens and multifamily buildings where grease and heavy use load the drains. A shared line serving several units backs up for everyone at once, so jetting on a schedule beats waiting for an emergency.

Hydro Jetting questions in Pleasantville

Can jetting damage my pipes?

On sound pipe it is safe. On cracked, sagging or brittle pipe it can do harm, which is why we run a camera before jetting and choose the pressure to suit the pipe.

How long does the cleaning last?

It depends on the cause. Grease control and root growth decide the interval, so ask us after the camera pass what schedule suits your line.

Is jetting only for commercial buildings?

No. Homes with repeat main line clogs benefit too, particularly where old pipe collects scale or roots enter at joints.

Do I need a cleanout for jetting?

A cleanout makes access easy. Without one, we may use a pulled toilet or a roof vent, and we can discuss installing a cleanout during the visit.

Do you handle hydro jetting emergencies at night or on weekends in Pleasantville?

Yes. Sewer backups are among the most common night calls, and our 24/7 line answers on weekends and holidays. We dispatch the same day when a plumber is available, and we tell you to stop using water while you wait.
