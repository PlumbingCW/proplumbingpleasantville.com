Absecon's older homes and what they mean for plumbing

Absecon became a city in 1902, and its own records list about 167 buildings in the local historic inventory, with parts of North and South Shore Road recognized as historic districts. Houses of that age often still carry original cast iron drain lines and mixed supply pipe.

If you own one, a camera look at the main drain is the cheapest way to learn its condition before a backup forces the question. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for these homes, including holidays.

Winter and spring checks for Absecon homes

Absecon winters can freeze pipes in unheated crawl spaces and garages, especially on older houses with little insulation. Wrap exposed lines, keep cabinet doors open on cold nights and know where your main valve is before the first freeze.

In spring, check the water heater's base and the washer hoses, since slow leaks show up as stains rather than puddles. A ten-minute walk-through often catches a problem before it becomes a repair bill, and our leak detection service confirms a loss you cannot see.
