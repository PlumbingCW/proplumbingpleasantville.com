Is it a pipe or the ground?

Check the water heater, the washer hoses and any exposed pipes for drips. Close the main valve for ten minutes and watch the meter. If the meter stops and the water keeps coming, the source is groundwater.

Water that appears after rain along the floor edge or at a wall crack points to groundwater, and water at a pipe or a fixture points to plumbing.

Signs of groundwater

  • Dampness at the floor and wall joint after rain.
  • White mineral deposits on masonry.
  • A musty smell that lingers.
  • Water through a floor crack.
  • Rust on the bottom of metal items stored on the floor.

Local conditions that raise the risk

Pleasantville's flood information names tidal flooding on its eastern and northern edges and creek flooding in the north. Properties in those areas can see a high water table after storms.

If your house is in one of those areas, expect seepage after heavy rain, and plan on a pump and an interior drain rather than a sealant alone.

Where plumbing fits in

A plumber handles the pump, the drains and the discharge piping, and checks the floor drain for a backwater valve. A floor drain connected to the sewer can also back up in a storm, and a sewer line cleaning visit may be part of the answer.

We pair the interior drain with a sump pump sized for the water it must move. For structural cracks or foundation movement, we recommend an engineer, and we say so plainly.

What we do not do

We do not repair foundation walls or perform structural work. If we see signs of foundation movement, we tell you and suggest you call a structural specialist.

What to do before and after a storm

Before a forecast storm, clear gutters and downspouts, test the sump pump and move belongings off the floor. Know where the main valve and the breaker panel are in case you need to shut things off quickly.

After a storm, check the floor drain, the pump and any damp spots. Photograph what you see, dry the area within a day or two and call us if water returned or the pump struggled.

Basement Waterproofing questions in Pleasantville

How do I tell a plumbing leak from seepage?

Close the main valve and watch the meter. If the meter stops moving but water continues, the source is groundwater.

Will an interior drain stop all water?

It collects water that reaches the footing and sends it to the pump, which keeps the floor dry. It does not stop groundwater from reaching the wall.

Do you seal foundation cracks?

We can seal minor cracks as part of a drainage plan. A growing or stair-step crack needs a structural opinion.

Is waterproofing an emergency?

Active flooding is. A damp wall that appears after rain can wait for a scheduled visit.
