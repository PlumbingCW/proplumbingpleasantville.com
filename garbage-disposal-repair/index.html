Leak under the sink: what to do first

Turn off the disposal switch, then unplug it if it is cord-connected, or switch off its breaker. Never put your hand in a disposal. Close the shutoff valves under the sink and set a bucket below the leak.

Take a photo of where the water is coming from. A leak at the top flange, the side hose or the bottom of the housing means different repairs, and a clear photo speeds the call.

Common disposal problems

  • A humming unit that will not turn, usually a jam.
  • A unit that is dead because of a tripped reset button.
  • A leak at the top ring where the sink meets the disposal.
  • A leak at the dishwasher hose or the drain pipe.
  • A bottom leak, which means the housing has failed.
  • A clog that backs water into the sink or the dishwasher.

A bottom leak is the one repair that is almost never worth doing. A cracked housing means the unit is finished, and a new disposal costs less than repeated attempts to fix an old one.

Installing a new disposal

Disposals vary in horsepower, noise and grinding capacity. A household that cooks often does better with a higher horsepower unit, and a continuous-feed model suits most kitchens.

We handle the mounting, the electrical connection, the dishwasher hose and the drain trap. If your sink has a double bowl, we also check the tailpiece layout so the drain flows freely.

When the clog is past the disposal

If water backs up in the sink even with the disposal running, the blockage may sit in the kitchen branch line. Our drain cleaning team handles that, and a grease-heavy line may need jetting.

Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation questions in Pleasantville

Why does my disposal hum but not spin?

The grinding plate is jammed. Never reach inside. A plumber frees it with a hex wrench at the bottom of the unit or a hand tool from above.

What should never go in a disposal?

Grease, coffee grounds in large amounts, fibrous peels, bones and starchy foods that swell. They coat the pipe or jam the blades.

Can you install a disposal where I never had one?

Yes. We check for an electrical outlet or switch, the drain layout and cabinet space before the install.

My dishwasher drains slowly. Is that the disposal?

It can be. The dishwasher drain connects to the disposal, and a blocked knockout plug or a clogged line backs water into the dishwasher.

Do you handle garbage disposal emergencies at night or on weekends in Pleasantville?

Yes. Disposal leaks and floods in the kitchen cabinet can happen at night, and our 24/7 line answers nights and weekends. We dispatch the same day when a plumber is available, and we tell you how to shut off the power and the sink valves.
