Our dispatch line covers Pleasantville and five nearby towns in Atlantic County. Each town has its own page with local conditions, ZIP codes and what to do before we arrive.

Pleasantville, ZIP 08232

Pleasantville is our home base. The city includes Mount Pleasant, Risleyville and Smiths Landing, and it borders Absecon, Atlantic City, Egg Harbor Township, Northfield and Ventnor City. See our homepage for the full list of services.

Tidal and creek flooding affect parts of the city, so sump pumps and sewer lines matter. Ask the dispatcher for a plumber who knows your area.

Water and sewer utilities

New Jersey American Water's Atlantic system lists Absecon, Egg Harbor Township, Linwood, Northfield and Pleasantville among the communities it serves. Ventnor City is not on that list, so check your bill or ask the city who supplies your water.

If a leak is on the utility's side of the meter, we tell you who to call and still help with the house side.

What to do before we arrive

Close the main valve and switch off power to wet areas. Move belongings off the floor and take photos for your insurer.

Then call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher your town, your street and what failed.

Absecon, north of Pleasantville

Absecon became a city in 1902 and has historic districts along Shore Road. Older houses there often carry original drain lines, so a camera check of the main drain is a sensible first step. See our Absecon page for details.

Northfield and Linwood, to the south

Northfield and Linwood are residential cities with long-owned homes. Water heaters and supply pipe often reach the end of their life together, so we look at both when we visit. The Northfield and Linwood pages explain local conditions.

Egg Harbor Township, to the west

The township covers about 75 square miles and many communities, so response time depends on where you are. Tell the dispatcher your cross streets and the nearest main road. The Egg Harbor Township page has the details.

Response across the area

Nearby neighborhoods in Pleasantville and Northfield are usually quickest to reach. Egg Harbor Township and Ventnor City can take longer depending on traffic and bridge openings, and the dispatcher gives you an honest window.

If water is escaping, do not wait for the plumber to arrive before you act. Close the main valve and switch off power to the wet area, and we will talk you through the rest.

Choosing the right page

Each town page lists the ZIP codes we cover, the local conditions that affect plumbing and what to do before we arrive. If you live between two towns, either page applies and the same 24/7 line answers.

If your street is not listed, call anyway, even if it sits between two of these towns or just beyond them. Ventnor City, on the barrier island

Ventnor sits only about three feet above sea level, so storm flooding and sewer backups are the main risks. Seasonal homes can leak unnoticed while empty. See the Ventnor City page for what to do.
