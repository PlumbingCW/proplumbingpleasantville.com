Sewer Inspection in Pleasantville, NJ

A sewer camera inspection shows what is happening inside your line without digging. We run a waterproof camera through the pipe in Pleasantville, NJ homes and businesses and record what we find, so repairs are based on footage rather than guesses.

A sewer inspection is a visual survey of the drain line from a cleanout or fixture to the street connection, using a camera on a flexible rod.

What the camera shows

The camera travels the line and records the pipe's material, joints, cracks, roots and low spots. It also finds grease buildup, scale and foreign objects that caused earlier clogs.

A locator in the camera head sends a signal we trace above ground. That tells us the depth and exact route of a problem so any digging stays small.

Common findings

  • Tree roots at joints or through cracks.
  • A belly where the pipe sags and holds waste.
  • Cracked, offset or separated joints.
  • Collapsed or crushed sections.
  • Heavy grease or scale on the pipe wall.
  • A cleanout that is missing or buried.

After a flood or storm

Pleasantville's own flood information points to tidal flooding from Lakes Bay and creek flooding in the north of the city. Saturated soil can shift pipes, and floodwater can push silt and debris into a line through cracked joints.

If your street flooded and a drain slowed after that, schedule a camera visit before problems compound. Our sewer line repair page explains the next step if damage turns up.

What we will and will not tell you

We report what the camera shows. We will not recommend a repair that the footage does not support, and we will say plainly if the line looks sound. You keep the video as your record.

What you receive after the inspection

You see the footage while we are there, and we explain each defect in plain terms. We tell you where it sits along the line and how serious it is.

We then lay out the options in order of cost and disruption, from cleaning to lining to digging. You decide what to do, and a clear price comes before any repair starts.

What affects the price of a sewer inspection

  • Length of the line to inspect.
  • Access through a cleanout or a pulled toilet.
  • Cleaning needed before the camera can pass.
  • Locating and marking the line above ground.
  • Report or video copy for a buyer or insurer.

Insurance generally does not pay for an inspection that is not tied to a claim, and a pre-purchase camera visit is usually a buyer's cost.

Sewer Inspection questions in Pleasantville

How long does a camera inspection take?

Most take under an hour. A line that needs cleaning first takes longer.

Can the camera tell me where the pipe is?

Yes. The camera head carries a locator, so we can mark the pipe's route and depth on the surface.

Do you give me the video?

Yes. You keep a copy as a record for your files, a buyer or an insurer.

What if the camera finds a blockage?

We can clear it through hydro jetting or cabling and then re-inspect the line.
