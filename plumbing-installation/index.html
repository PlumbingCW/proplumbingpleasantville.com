What we install

Common installations

  • Sinks, faucets and toilets.
  • Tubs, showers and valves.
  • Dishwasher and washing machine hookups.
  • Water filters and pressure regulators.
  • Hose bibs and outdoor spigots.
  • Shutoff valves and supply lines.
  • Added bathrooms and kitchen lines.

Good installation starts with the rough-in. We check the supply, the drain slope and the vent before we set anything, because a fixture that fits badly never drains well.

If you bought the fixture yourself, send us the model. We check that it fits your rough-in distance and supply before the visit.

Water heaters and larger fixtures

Large installs have their own pages. See water heater installation for tank and tankless units, and toilet repair for new toilets. This page covers everything else, from a new kitchen sink to a washer hookup.

Permits and code

New Jersey licenses master plumbers through the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, and many installations need a municipal permit and inspection. We tell you at the start whether your job needs one.

Permit rules vary by town and by the scope of work, so we confirm with your local building department before we start.

Installing without a plan

A fixture connected to the wrong pipe size, or a drain without a vent, may work for a week and then fail. We check the whole run when we install, not just the fixture.

How a typical installation visit goes

We confirm the fixture, check the rough-in and shut off the water at the right valve. Then we set the new fixture, connect supply and drain, and turn the water back on slowly to check every joint.

We test the fixture under normal use and leave the space clean. If we find a problem behind the wall, we stop, explain it and give you the price to fix it before going further.

Standards and sources

  • Plumbing work in New Jersey is governed by the Uniform Construction Code plumbing subcode (N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15), which adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code. Your local construction official issues permits and inspects the work.
