Sewer backup: first steps

Stop every drain in the house, since each flush pushes more wastewater toward the weak point. Photograph affected areas for your insurer, wear gloves and boots if you must enter the space, and keep children and pets away.

Call the 24/7 line before you start cleanup. Our plumber needs the line untouched to see exactly what the camera shows.

Signs of a damaged sewer line

  • Multiple drains backing up at the same time.
  • A sewage smell in the yard or the lowest room.
  • A wet patch or sinkhole in the lawn along the pipe path.
  • Gurgling toilets after a nearby flush.
  • Drain cleanings that fail within weeks.
  • Slow drains that worsen after heavy rain.

Who owns which part of the line

In most places the homeowner owns the pipe from the house to the street connection, and the municipality owns the main in the street. Rules differ, so ask Pleasantville's public works office who is responsible for the section between your property line and the main.

That question matters before any digging. A repair under a public street or sidewalk may need permits, and we can describe the work so the office can answer quickly.

Low ground, pump stations and storm stress

A state post-Sandy planning report on Pleasantville lists four sanitary sewer pump stations, at Greenfield Avenue, Plaza Avenue, Cedar Crest Avenue and Doughty Road, for which the city sought emergency generators after the October 2012 storm. It shows how much of the city's sewer system depends on pumping in low ground.

For a homeowner that means heavy rain and high tides stress both the city system and your lateral. A line with cracks takes on groundwater, and a line with sags holds waste during surges. A camera look after any major storm is cheap insurance, and our sewer inspection page explains the visit.

Sewer Line Repair questions in Pleasantville

Do I need to dig up my whole yard?

Often not. Spot repairs open one small area, and lining or bursting work from short access pits instead of a long trench.

What causes sewer lines to crack?

Age, soil movement, tree roots, heavy loads and old material such as clay or fiber pipe. A camera inspection shows which one applies.

How long does a repair take?

A spot repair can be finished in a day. Lining or full replacement takes longer, and we give a time estimate once we see the camera footage.

Can I still use my plumbing during repairs?

Usually not while the line is open. We tell you how long to expect an outage, and our sewer line cleaning visit can help if the line is only blocked.

Do you handle sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Pleasantville?

Yes. Active sewage backups are treated as emergencies and our 24/7 line answers nights and weekends. We dispatch the same day when a plumber is available, and we tell you to stop using water and keep away from standing sewage.
