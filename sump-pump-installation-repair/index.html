Pump stopped during a storm: what to do

Check the plug and the breaker first. If the pump hums but does not move water, the impeller may be jammed or the float stuck. If it does nothing at all, the motor or switch has failed.

Do not reach into standing water if the pump or outlets are submerged. Water and electricity together can be lethal, so call us and, if water is near the panel, call your electric utility.

Common sump pump failures

  • A float that sticks in the down position.
  • A motor that burned out from running too long.
  • A check valve that fails and sends water back into the pit.
  • A frozen or blocked discharge line.
  • A power outage with no battery backup.
  • A pit that is too small, so the pump cycles constantly.

Why Pleasantville basements need dependable pumps

The city's own flood information describes tidal flooding on its eastern and northern edges and creek flooding in the north. Those same conditions raise the water table after heavy rain, which pushes groundwater toward crawl spaces and basements.

If your home sits in a low area, a pump that fails once will fail again at the worst time. A newer pump with a backup is cheaper than a flooded floor.

Battery backup and maintenance

Storms often bring outages, which stop the primary pump at the moment it is needed. A battery backup runs the second pump for a limited time, and it needs a battery check each year.

Test the pump at the start of each wet season. Pour a bucket of water into the pit, watch the float rise and listen for the pump to run and then shut off.

A pump moves water, it does not seal a basement

If the pump runs constantly or walls seep, the cause is outside the pit. See our basement waterproofing page for drainage and sealing work.

Sump Pump Installation and Repair questions in Pleasantville

How long should a sump pump last?

Many last about ten years, depending on how often they run. A pump that cycles often wears faster.

Do I need a battery backup?

If your area loses power in storms, yes. The primary pump stops when the power goes, and the backup keeps the pit from filling.

Where should the discharge line empty?

Away from the foundation and not toward a neighbor's property. We check the route and the slope during the install.

My pump runs but the basement is still wet. Why?

The pump may be undersized or the check valve may be failing. Water may also be seeping from places the pit does not drain, which is a waterproofing issue.

Do you handle sump pump emergencies at night or on weekends in Pleasantville?

Yes. Pumps fail during storms, which happen at any hour, and our 24/7 line answers nights and weekends. We dispatch the same day when a plumber is available and tell you how to protect belongings while you wait.
