When a clogged drain is an emergency

Most single-sink clogs are an annoyance, not a crisis. The line changes when several fixtures slow down together, when a basement floor drain bubbles, or when a toilet flush makes the tub gurgle. Those signs point to a blockage in the main drain, which carries every fixture in the house.

Stop using water if you see any of that. Every extra gallon has nowhere to go and may surface at the lowest opening in the house. Call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher which fixtures are affected so the right cable and camera go on the truck.

Signs your drain needs cleaning now

  • Water drains slowly from more than one fixture.
  • A gurgling sound from a tub or sink after a toilet flush.
  • Sewage smell near a floor drain or the lowest bathroom.
  • Water pooling around the shower base while the washer drains.
  • A clog that comes back within a few weeks of plunging.

How we clear the line

We start by finding where the blockage sits. A clog at one trap is cleared through the fixture. A clog past the last branch needs access through a cleanout, a pipe in the basement or crawl space, or the roof vent.

A cable with a cutting head works for soft blockages such as hair, soap and paper. Roots and scale need a heavier head, and grease coating a long run often needs hydro jetting to clean the full pipe wall instead of poking one hole through it.

Kitchen sink clogs

Kitchen lines clog differently from bathroom lines because cooking fat cools inside the pipe and traps food scraps. A sink that drains a little slower each month is usually coated with grease rather than blocked at one point. A disposal that hums but does not clear may also have a jammed line behind it, which is covered on our garbage disposal repair page.

Drain problems that follow Pleasantville weather

Pleasantville sits about seven feet above sea level, and the city's flood information describes tidal flooding from Lakes Bay and Conovers Creek. During a hard rain or a high tide, low yards and streets hold water, and a drain that was barely flowing can stop altogether.

That timing matters. If the problem appears during a storm, check whether the water is rising only inside the house or also in the yard before you call. Either way, a backed-up main in a flood-prone block needs a camera look, since a clog and a damaged line can feel identical from the sink.

When it is not the drain at all

A drain that clears and then clogs again within days often has a belly or a crack in the line holding waste in one place. A camera visit costs less than repeated cleanings. We cover that step on our sewer inspection page and the repair choices under sewer line repair.

Drain Cleaning questions in Pleasantville

Why does my drain keep clogging after I clear it?

Repeated clogs usually mean grease or scale has narrowed the pipe, or the line has a low spot that holds waste. A camera inspection shows which. Cabling opens a hole through the blockage, while jetting cleans the whole pipe wall.

Can I use chemical drain cleaner first?

We advise against it. Chemical cleaners can sit in a standing line and splash on whoever opens the pipe afterward. They also fail on solid blockages such as roots and wipes, so a plunger or a plumber is the safer path.

Is a slow bathroom sink the same as a main line clog?

Not usually. One slow sink points to its own trap or branch. If the tub, toilet and a floor drain are all slow, the main line is the likelier cause.

Do you clean drains for landlords and small businesses?

Yes. We handle rental units, shops and small offices, and our commercial plumbing page explains how after-hours drain calls are handled for them.
