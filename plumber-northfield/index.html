Northfield by neighborhood

Northfield covers 3.6 square miles, and its unincorporated areas include Bakersville and Dolphin. The city counts 3,309 housing units, which means most calls come from single-family homes with their own water heaters and basements.

Dispatch is available around the clock, and a short drive from Pleasantville keeps response quick across the whole city.

Birch Grove Park, a 271-acre wooded park with fishing lakes, sits near wet ground. Homes near parkland and open water can see a higher water table after storms, so a sump pump is worth checking before the wet season.

A pump that has not been tested in a year is the one that fails on the night of a nor'easter, and our sump pump service covers repair and backup installs.

Heavy rain and low spots

Northfield is bordered by Egg Harbor Township, Linwood and Pleasantville. During heavy rain, storm drains clog with leaves and silt, and low yards hold water near foundations.

Keep the street drain in front of your home clear, and tell us if your main line backs up only in storms. That pattern points to a lateral that is close to full.

Before the wet season in Northfield

Check the pump, clear the gutters and make sure downspouts empty well away from the foundation. These small steps keep groundwater from reaching a basement floor, and they cost little compared with a flooded finished space.

If you have a floor drain, pour water into it and confirm it flows freely. A floor drain that is slow now will back up in a storm.

What to tell the dispatcher

Give your street, the room that is affected and whether water is still running. Mention if the house has a finished basement or a sump pit, because that changes the equipment we bring.
