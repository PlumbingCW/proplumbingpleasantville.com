The guides are written for local conditions, including Pleasantville's flood areas and its water system. Each one links to the matching service page and the nearby towns.

Water heater guides

Four guides cover hot water. One explains the thermocouple that keeps a gas pilot lit, one explains what a hybrid gas water heater is, and two cover tankless units: the installation requirements and the exhaust pipe.

Read them before you buy a new heater, because gas size, vent routes and drains decide the cost more than the unit itself.

Leak, drain and sewer guides

Two guides help you find hidden water. One walks through a meter test for a yard leak, and the other covers the signs of a slab leak. A third explains why sewer lining does not price cleanly per foot.

Start with the meter test if your bill has risen without a reason, since it takes minutes and costs nothing.

Basement and sump pump guides

Wet basements are common near low ground and tidal areas. One guide separates what a homeowner can do about exterior waterproofing from what needs a professional, and another walks through a submersible sump pump install.

Both begin by ruling out a plumbing leak, because no coating or pump fixes a pipe that is losing water.

Fixture guides

Three guides cover everyday fixtures. They explain how toilet valves fail, what drives faucet repair prices and who can install a garbage disposal.

These are the jobs where a small repair now avoids a larger one later, so they are worth a quick read before you call.

How we write these guides

Each guide explains what to do first, how the repair works and what affects the price. We do not publish fixed prices, because the right price depends on the house.

Local notes draw on public sources such as the city's flood information and the water utility's reports, and we say plainly when a topic needs a different specialist.
