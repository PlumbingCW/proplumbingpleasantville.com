Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you call or visit this site.

Calls to our dispatch line may be recorded.

This policy explains what Pleasantville Pro Plumbing collects when you use this website or call our dispatch line. We keep it short because we collect little.

What we collect

When you call, we collect your phone number, your address and a description of the problem so we can send a plumber. Calls to the dispatch line may be recorded, and we tell you at the start of the call.

This website has no forms, so we do not collect information through it. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested.

Call recording notice

Calls to our dispatch line may be recorded for quality, training and to document what was agreed. By continuing the call after the notice, you agree to the recording. If you do not agree, tell the dispatcher and end the call.

How we use information

We use call information to dispatch a plumber, give you a price, document the work and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share the details a plumber needs to reach you and do the work. We may also share information when the law requires it or to protect safety.

Third-party content

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows maps from Google Maps. Those services may collect technical data such as your IP address under their own policies.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete a call record or tell us not to contact you again by calling the dispatch line or writing to contact@proplumbingpleasantville.com.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date below when we do. Last updated November 14, 2026.
