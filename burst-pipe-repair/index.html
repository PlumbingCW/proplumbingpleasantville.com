What to do when a pipe bursts: the first five minutes

Shut the main

Find the main valve near the meter or water heater and close it fully.

Cut the power

Switch off breakers to wet rooms if water is near outlets or the panel.

Drain the line

Open the lowest faucet and a high one to release pressure.

Move belongings

Lift furniture and electronics out of the wet area and photograph the damage.

Call

Reach the 24/7 line and tell us where the water is coming from.

Do not wait to see if the leak slows. A burst line pushes water into walls and ceilings the whole time it is under pressure, and the damage grows fast.

Frozen pipes in Pleasantville

Pipes in unheated spaces such as crawl spaces, garages and exterior walls freeze first. When a pipe freezes but has not burst, shut off the main, open the nearest faucet and warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer. Never use an open flame.

A frozen pipe that bursts often shows the damage only when it thaws. If the faucets are dry in cold weather, treat it as a likely burst and call us before the thaw.

Older pipe materials

Copper repairs often use sweated fittings or press fittings. Galvanized steel corrodes from the inside, and a burst section often means the rest is close behind. Cast iron drain pipe cracks across or splits along the length and is repaired with a coupling or a section replacement.

Where the same line fails twice, we explain whether a longer repair or a repiping project is more sensible. A sudden leak in a line you did not know about is also a job for leak detection.

Floodwater is not a burst pipe

Pleasantville's tidal and creek flooding can leave water in yards and crawl spaces after a storm. If the main valve is closed and water keeps appearing, the source is outside. Contact the city for flooding, and call us when a pipe is the problem.

Burst Pipe Repair questions in Pleasantville

How do I find my main water shutoff?

Look near the water meter, where the line enters the house, or near the water heater. Learn its location before an emergency, and test that it turns.

Will insurance cover a burst pipe?

Often it covers sudden water damage but not gradual leaks or the pipe itself. Read your policy or ask your agent.

How soon can the pipe be repaired?

Many repairs finish the same day. Larger sections or hard-to-reach pipes can take longer, and we tell you before work begins.

Can you help with drying?

We handle the plumbing. Drying and surface repairs are a separate trade, and we can describe what we found so your restoration company has the details.
