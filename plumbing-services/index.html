Not sure what you need?

Describe the symptom to the dispatcher. A single slow drain points to drain cleaning, while several slow drains point to sewer line cleaning. No hot water points to water heater repair, and a damp basement after rain may need a sump pump.

We tell you honestly when a problem is outside plumbing, such as foundation cracks or burner and gas work on a boiler.

How to choose a plumber

Ask for a clear price before work starts, and confirm the plumber is licensed through the New Jersey State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers. Ask what is included and what could change the price.

A good plumber explains the problem in plain terms and tells you if a repair is not worth doing.

Pricing and what is included

Each page lists the factors that change the price of that service, so you know what to expect. We confirm the price before work starts and tell you if anything changes.

What a typical visit looks like

A dispatcher takes your address and the symptoms, then tells you what to shut off. A plumber arrives with the tools for the job, diagnoses the problem and gives a clear price before starting.

After the repair, the plumber tests the work and explains what was done. If something else needs attention later, we tell you now and let you choose when.
