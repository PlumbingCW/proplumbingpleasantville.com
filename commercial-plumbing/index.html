What to do during an after-hours plumbing emergency

A leak above a ceiling, a flooded restroom or a backed-up kitchen drain all put revenue and safety at risk. Shut off the water at the main or the fixture, switch off power to wet equipment, and photograph the damage for your insurer.

Tell the dispatcher the building type, how many fixtures are affected and whether you lease or own. That shapes the equipment sent and whether the landlord needs notice.

Common calls from commercial customers

  • Restroom leaks and running toilets in shared spaces.
  • Kitchen drains and grease buildup in food service.
  • Floor drains that overflow after a shift.
  • Water heater failures that stop hot water for staff.
  • Backflow assemblies that need annual testing.
  • Leaks in suspended ceilings over inventory.

Landlords, tenants and property managers

If you manage rentals or lease space, who pays for the plumbing depends on your lease. We bill the party you tell us is responsible, and we document the problem with photos and a written description for your records.

For multifamily buildings, a shared drain line backs up units at once. Our hydro jetting service and routine cleaning visits prevent the repeat calls.

Backflow testing

A backflow preventer protects the public water supply from contamination. Many commercial properties must have these devices tested on a schedule set by the water provider or local code, so ask the water supplier what applies to your building. We can test and repair common assemblies and give you the documentation your provider asks for.

Grease trap and storm drain service

Kitchens that discharge grease need a grease trap or interceptor that is cleaned on a regular schedule. A full trap sends grease into the sewer, which clogs both the building line and the main. We service traps and clean the lines that feed them.

Parking lots and loading areas have storm drains that clog with leaves and silt. A blocked storm drain floods a lot or a loading dock during heavy rain, and cleaning it before storm season costs far less than the cleanup.
