How to reach us

Call the number above and tell the dispatcher your town, your street and the problem. We do not use contact forms, so a call is the quickest route.

For non-urgent questions you can also write to contact@proplumbingpleasantville.com, and we reply during business hours.

Before you call

Have this ready

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • What failed and when it started.
  • Whether you have closed the main valve.
  • The age of the water heater or the affected pipe if you know it.

Hours and response

The line is open 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays. We dispatch the same day when a plumber is available, and the dispatcher gives you a realistic arrival window.

Response time depends on your location, traffic and earlier calls. We update you if the window changes.

Emergency or scheduled work

Call right away for a burst pipe, a sewer backup, no hot water in cold weather or water near electrical equipment. For a slow drain, a dripping faucet or a planned install, call and we schedule a convenient visit.

If you are unsure which applies, describe the symptoms and the dispatcher will help you decide.
