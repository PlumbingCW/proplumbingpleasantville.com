Water heater emergency: what to do first

A leak from the tank body is the one failure repair cannot fix. Water seeping from a fitting, valve or the temperature and pressure relief line is another story and can often be repaired on the spot.

Switch off the power at the breaker for an electric unit. For gas, turn the gas control to pilot or off. Then close the cold supply valve above the heater and open a hot tap somewhere in the house to relieve pressure.

Symptoms we diagnose most often

  • No hot water at all, or only lukewarm water.
  • Hot water that runs out after a few minutes.
  • A rumbling or popping noise from the tank.
  • Rust-colored hot water but clear cold water.
  • Water on the floor around the base or pooling under the pan.
  • A pilot light that will not stay lit.

Gas and electric tanks fail in different ways

Common failures by heater type
Heater typeFrequent failureWhat the repair usually involves
Electric tankBurned out lower or upper heating elementDrain the tank and replace the element and thermostat.
Gas tankDirty burner or failing thermocoupleClean the burner and replace the thermocouple or gas valve.
Any tankSediment buildup on the tank floorFlush the tank and test the heating parts.
Any tankLeaking relief valve or drain valveReplace the valve and check the pressure on the line.

A failed thermocouple is a good example of an inexpensive repair that is easy to misread. The pilot flame goes out because the safety sensor is worn, not because the heater is finished. If your pilot will not stay lit, that part is a likely cause, and a replacement takes far less time than a new unit.

Repair or replace the tank

Age and the leak location decide this. A heater that is ten or more years old and leaking from the tank has reached the end of its life, and repair money is better spent on water heater replacement. A newer heater with a failed part is usually worth repairing.

Ask us to inspect the anode rod, since it is the sacrificial part that protects the tank steel. A worn rod is a cheap fix that can add years, and our team can check it during the same visit.

Water Heater Repair questions in Pleasantville

How long does a water heater repair take?

Most element, thermostat and valve repairs finish in one visit. A part that must be ordered adds time, and we will tell you when that is the case.

My water heater is leaking from the top. Is that serious?

A leak at the top is often a loose fitting or the relief valve, which are repairable. Turn off the power and the cold water supply, then call us so we can see where the water is actually coming from.

Why is my hot water brown?

Rust from the tank or from old iron pipes is the usual cause. If only the hot side is discolored, the tank or anode rod is the likelier source and should be inspected.

Can you also install the next one?

Yes. If the old unit is beyond repair, we move straight to water heater installation and size the new tank to your household.

Do you handle water heater emergencies at night or on weekends in Pleasantville?

Yes. Water heater failures often happen at night, and our line answers 24/7. We dispatch the same day when a plumber is available, and the first instruction we give is to switch the heater off and close the cold water valve.
