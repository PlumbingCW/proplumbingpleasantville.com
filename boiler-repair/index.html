No heat: what to do and what to check

Check the thermostat

Make sure it is set to heat and the batteries are fresh.

Look at the pressure gauge

Most systems run around 12 to 15 psi when cold. A gauge near zero points to a leak, and a gauge in the red points to an expansion problem.

Check for water

Look under and around the boiler for drips or puddles.

Smell for gas

If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility and 911 first.

Call us

Give the boiler age, the fuel type and what you saw.

If you smell gas or a boiler shows signs of combustion trouble, such as soot or a carbon monoxide alarm, leave the building and call 911 first. We handle plumbing and water-side repairs, and burner and gas work belongs with a licensed heating contractor.

Older heating systems

Many boilers in established neighborhoods run for decades. Old piping, original valves and rusted radiators fail more often as the system ages, and a repair that makes sense on a young boiler may not on a very old one.

We tell you honestly when repairs stack up. A boiler that needs a new circulator, a new valve and a leak repair in the same year may be telling you it is time to plan a replacement with a heating contractor.

Boiler and hot water

Some homes make domestic hot water with the boiler and others use a separate tank. If you lose heat and hot water together, the boiler is the likely cause. If only hot water fails, see our water heater repair page.

Frozen heating pipes

If the boiler fails during a hard freeze, the heating pipes can freeze and burst. Open the nearest faucet to keep water moving, and read about burst pipe repair if you see water.

Boiler Repair questions in Pleasantville

What pressure should my boiler show?

Most residential boilers sit near 12 to 15 psi when cold, but check your manual. Pressure well outside the normal range needs service.

Do you repair the burner and gas side?

We handle water-side and plumbing repairs. Burner, gas and combustion work belongs with a licensed heating contractor, and we tell you when we see signs of it.

Why does my boiler make banging noises?

Scale buildup or low flow can cause it. A flush often helps, and we check the pump.

Should I repair or replace an old boiler?

If repairs keep stacking up, replacement planning makes sense. We explain what we found so you can decide with a heating contractor.
