Storm season in Egg Harbor Township

The township covers 75 square miles, and 11 percent of it is water. That much shoreline means groundwater and tidal flooding are part of life, and sump pumps and floor drains work hard in a nor'easter.

Before the wet months, pour a bucket of water into your sump pit and confirm the pump starts. A pump that fails once will usually fail again, and our sump pump page explains the repair and backup options.

A township of many communities

Within the township are Bargaintown, Cardiff, English Creek, Farmington, Scullville, Steelmanville and West Atlantic City. Each has its own mix of housing ages, from older cottages to newer subdivisions.

That mix means we ask the age of the house first. A newer home with PEX and a tankless unit has different failure points than an older home with copper and a tank, and we plan the visit around it.

Newer homes still need maintenance

Subdivision homes built in recent decades often use PEX supply lines and plastic drains. Those materials last, but fittings can loosen and water heaters still reach the end of their life.

Check the water heater's age label and the temperature and pressure valve once a year, and ask us if the unit is near ten years old.
